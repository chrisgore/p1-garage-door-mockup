Quick diagnosis

What's your door doing?

Likely cause

Broken torsion spring

The spring above the door carries its weight. When one snaps it sounds like a gunshot, and the opener can't lift the door on its own.

Typical fix
Replace both springs as a matched pair, then rebalance
Typical range
$250 – $450

Safety: don't try to lift the door or pull the red release cord while it's open. Leave it closed and call.
